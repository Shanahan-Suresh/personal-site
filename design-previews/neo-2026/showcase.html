Your four favourites, improved

You loved four designs in the first round: 2 Product, 3 Editorial, 5 Desktop and 10 Poster. Each one is rebuilt here around your note on it. They also all aim for the same target: a site that looks built by a senior web developer.

Each design has a switch to show its first version, so you can see what changed.
