# Homepage media

The homepage uses seven real project previews, four licensed camera photographs and a cutout of the owner's retouched headshot. Neither mascot pose appears on a page. Astro emits responsive WebP variants with explicit dimensions. The hero portrait and the RSVP capture beside it load eagerly; other media loads lazily. Captions remain accessible HTML.

## Hero composition

The hero pairs two frames on the sky slab. The large frame is `scrapbook/portrait-cutout-v4.png` on a flat cream ground (`--cream`, light in both themes), a 4:5 `cover` crop at `50% 18%`. The small frame overlaps its lower-right corner: `work/rsvp-glass-hero.png`, the same RSVP mock-up rendered at 16:11 so both cards sit whole in the frame, captioned with the RSVP guest count. Both sit in the ink `.frame` with a hard offset shadow. No photograph appears in the hero.

Project rows and story dialogs show the captures at their own proportions, which keeps QR codes, plot labels and controls legible. The interest photographs use square `cover` crops.

## Censorit artwork

`samples/cabanel-birth-of-venus-1875-v4.jpg` preserves the unaltered 4000 × 2371 image of [The Birth of Venus](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/435831), Alexandre Cabanel, 1875, oil on canvas, The Metropolitan Museum of Art, object 435831 (accession 94.24.1). Credit: Gift of John Wolfe, 1893. The museum identifies this photograph as public domain. It is Cabanel's 1875 replica of his 1863 composition.

The application applies supported Solid Fill `#172238` with zero padding to the adult Venus's chest `(2630, 1290, 2870, 1450)` and pelvic region `(1860, 1490, 2190, 1710)`. Manual regions use the source `EllipseSegmenter` capsule masks. The complete painting remains in frame. The source image is unaltered; the application produces all censorship. Receipt assertions verify fill pixels, unchanged unmasked pixels, two manual regions, zero automatic detections and unchanged source files.

## Portrait and retained assets

`scrapbook/portrait-studio-v2.png` is a built-in imagegen studio edit of the real headshot in `design-previews/mascot/me.png`, retaining the owner's navy jacket, white shirt and smile. The original photograph remains in `scrapbook/portrait.png`.

`scrapbook/portrait-cutout-v4.png` uses only the alpha channel from built-in imagegen's background extraction. Every RGB pixel is restored from `portrait-studio-v2.png` and verified byte-for-byte at 1122 × 1402. The active portrait receives no further facial retouch, colour or lighting change. The cream ground behind it is a flat CSS fill (`--cream`), and the ink frame and hard shadow come from the site's `.frame` style. The raw extraction candidate remains in `scrapbook/portrait-extraction-source-v4.png`.

Exact studio-edit and extraction prompts are in [generation-prompts.json](scrapbook/generation-prompts.json). The extraction record distinguishes the retained alpha from the unused generated RGB.

Unreferenced illustrations, editorial photographs, collages, both mascot poses and superseded captures remain on disk. Their prompts remain in [source-generation-prompts.json](scrapbook/source-generation-prompts.json), [photo-generation-prompts.json](scrapbook/photo-generation-prompts.json) and [collage-generation-prompts-v3.json](scrapbook/collage-generation-prompts-v3.json). Neither mascot is imported or rendered on a page.
